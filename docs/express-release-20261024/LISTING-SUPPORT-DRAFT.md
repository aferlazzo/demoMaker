## Proposed Store copy

**Name:** demoMaker Express (availability/reservation unverified)

**Short description:** Turn real screen recordings and your script into locally narrated Windows demonstration videos.

**Description:** Import a real application recording or use supported live capture. Write a short scene script, generate local American English narration, and create a captioned MP4. Choose a standard video or a vertical Reel layout. Preview before exporting and reuse your project settings next time. No paid voice API, narration subscription or account is required for local media processing after setup.

**Important comparison:** The core workflow is also available in the existing free demoMaker Windows installer. The proposed $19.99 Store edition principally offers Store delivery and updates, subject to certification. Do not describe narration, Reels, captions or the guided workflow as Express-exclusive. Do not claim the Store version exists until it is certified and published.

**Features supported by the current app:** script scenes; local Piper narration; measured-segment caption timing and SRT export; imported genuine MP4/WebM footage; supported screen/microphone capture; static crop/privacy masks; H.264/AAC export; 1080×1920 Reel layout; project JSON save/load. Hardware/permission behavior must be checked on supported Windows devices before paid release.

**Limits to disclose:** Windows x64 only in this readiness work; synthetic American English voice, not premium multilingual voice selection; no timeline editor, automatic zoom/cuts, transcription, dynamic secret detection or automatic social posting. Fixed masks do not follow moving information. Project files do not embed footage/generated audio. Save exports before closing; no crash recovery. Imported source/video limits and the 20-minute maximum remain. Never promise unlimited duration, lifetime future upgrades/support or professional voice quality.

**Proposed price:** $19.99 one-time. No paid listing or sale is authorized by this draft. Store price availability, markets and standard purchase terms require owner review.
